1 Who We Are

Synprism ("Synprism", "we", "us", or "our") is an AI integration and consulting firm headquartered in Tennessee, United States. We operate the website located at synprism.io (the "Site"), including all sub-pages such as our chatbot services page and chatbot intake questionnaire.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our Site or interact with us through our contact form, chatbot intake questionnaire, or any other features on the Site. Please read it carefully.

Short version: We collect information you voluntarily provide through our contact form and/or chatbot intake questionnaire. We use it to respond to you and deliver the service you requested. We don't sell it. Ever.

2 Information We Collect

A. Contact Form

When you submit our main contact form, we collect the following personally identifiable information ("PII"):

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Company or organization name (optional)
  • Service interest (the category you select)
  • Message content (what you write to us)

Submitting the contact form is entirely voluntary. You are not required to submit it to access our website.

B. Chatbot Intake Questionnaire Updated

Our chatbot services page includes a multi-step intake questionnaire for clients who wish to have a custom AI chatbot built for their business. If you choose to complete and submit this form, we collect the following information:

  • Your name / contact person
  • Email address

  • Business website URL (optional)
  • Business type (selected from a menu)
  • Business description

This information is collected solely to enable us to build your custom chatbot. Submission is voluntary - you are not required to complete this form to use our website. This data is delivered to our team via Web3Forms (see Section 4).

C. Information Collected Automatically Updated

When you visit our Site, certain data is collected automatically through the following technologies:

  • Cloudflare (CDN & Security) - Our hosting provider collects technical data including your IP address (used for DDoS mitigation and bot detection), browser type and version, pages visited and time spent, and referring website. This data is used solely for security and performance purposes by Cloudflare and is governed by Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

D. Information We Do NOT Collect

3 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiry - Contact form submissions are used solely to reply to your question or service interest.
  • To build your custom chatbot - Intake questionnaire data is used to design, configure, and deliver the chatbot you requested, including branding, service content, hours, and tier features.
  • To contact you about your project - We use your name, email, and phone number from the intake form to follow up, ask clarifying questions, and coordinate delivery of your chatbot.
  • To improve our services - Understanding what services people are interested in and how they navigate our site helps us improve our offerings and website experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations - We may use your information as required by applicable law.

We do not use your information for:

  • Unsolicited marketing, spam, or cold outreach unrelated to your inquiry
  • Automated profiling or algorithmic decision-making
  • Sale or rental to third parties
  • Targeted advertising or retargeting campaigns

Our legal basis for processing your information (under GDPR) is legitimate interest - specifically, responding to a business inquiry or delivering a service you have requested - and in some cases, your consent as implied by voluntarily submitting a form.

4 How We Share Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

Form Delivery - Web3Forms

Both our contact form and our chatbot intake questionnaire use the Web3Forms API to deliver submitted data to our email inbox. Web3Forms receives the fields you submit solely to transmit them to us. Web3Forms acts as a data processor on our behalf and does not store or use your data for any other purpose. Please review Web3Forms' Privacy Policy for details.

Infrastructure - Cloudflare

Our Site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages and our contact form backend runs as a Cloudflare Worker. All web traffic is processed through Cloudflare's global network for performance, security, and analytics. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Analytics - Google Analytics 4

Anonymized, aggregated website usage data is shared with Google for analytics purposes via Google Analytics 4. Google does not receive your name, email, phone, or any information submitted through our forms. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental authority, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.

Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you via email if such a transfer occurs and your data is subject to a materially different privacy policy.

5 Data Retention

We retain contact form and chatbot intake questionnaire submissions in our email inbox for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which they were collected - typically the duration of our business relationship or correspondence with you, plus a reasonable period thereafter for record-keeping (generally up to three years).

Chatbot intake data (including business information, branding details, and service content) may be retained as part of the project record for the chatbot we build for you, for the duration of our service relationship.

You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Your Rights below). Upon a valid deletion request, we will remove your information from our active systems within 30 days, except where we are legally required to retain it or where it is necessary to complete an ongoing engagement you initiated.

6 Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • HTTPS encryption on all pages (enforced via Cloudflare SSL/TLS)
  • Cloudflare WAF and DDoS protection on all traffic
  • Encrypted form transmission - form data is transmitted via encrypted HTTPS API calls to Web3Forms
  • No server-side database of form submissions - intake data is delivered directly to our email inbox and is not stored in a web-accessible database
  • Limited data scope - we collect only the information necessary to deliver the requested service

No method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach that affects your rights, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

10 Cookies & Analytics

We use a minimal cookie footprint. We do not use advertising cookies, behavioral tracking pixels, or retargeting technologies.

The following cookies and analytics technologies are active on this Site:

  • __cf_bm (Cloudflare Bot Management) - Set automatically by Cloudflare to distinguish human visitors from automated bots. This cookie expires within 30 minutes of inactivity, does not track you across websites, and contains no personal information. It is strictly necessary for the secure operation of our Site.
  • Google Analytics 4 cookies (_ga, _ga_*) - Set by GA4 to distinguish unique visitors and sessions. These cookies persist for up to 2 years. The GA4 cookies are non-essential. By continuing to use our Site, you acknowledge their presence. If you prefer not to be tracked by GA4, please use the opt-out tool linked above. For Cloudflare's cookie details, see Cloudflare's cookie documentation.

14 SMS Notifications New

Program Description

SynPrism Sale Alerts is a transactional SMS notification service. When an item sells on your connected storefront, we send a text message to alert you. This is not a marketing program - messages are limited to transactional sale event notifications sent only to store owners who have explicitly opted in.

Opt-In

You may enroll at synprism.io/sms-alerts by entering your name and mobile number and checking an unchecked consent box. The consent box discloses message purpose, variable frequency, applicable rates, and STOP/HELP instructions. Your opt-in is recorded with a timestamp and the exact consent language shown at submission. A number receives alerts only after a Synprism account owner approves it. A confirmation text is then sent to confirm enrollment.

Information Collected at Opt-In

  • Name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Consent timestamp and the exact consent wording presented

This data is used solely to deliver sale alert notifications and is not used for marketing or shared with third parties except as described below. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

Message Frequency & Content

Message frequency varies based on your store's sales activity. Each message contains sale event details (item sold, sale price, and shipping timeline). No promotional or marketing content is included in any message.

Msg & data rates may apply. Contact your carrier for details on applicable charges.

Opt-Out

Reply STOP to any message at any time to cancel. You will receive one confirmation message and no further alerts will be sent. To re-enroll, submit the opt-in form again.

Help

Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or email info@synprism.io.

SMS Service Provider - Twilio

SMS messages are delivered via Twilio, which acts as a data processor on our behalf. Twilio receives your mobile number and message content solely to transmit alerts. See Twilio's Privacy Policy. STOP/HELP opt-out keywords are handled by Twilio's default opt-out management.

Carrier Disclaimer

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Service availability may vary by carrier and geographic region.
